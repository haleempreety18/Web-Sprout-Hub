Certificate FAQs

Are the certificates free?

Yes! Every student who completes a course receives a certificate at no additional cost.

How long does it take to receive my certificate?

Certificates are issued within 3-5 business days after course completion and assessment approval.

Can employers verify my certificate?

Yes! Each certificate has a unique ID that can be verified by contacting us via email or phone.

What format will I receive my certificate in?

You'll receive a high-resolution PDF certificate that you can print or share digitally on LinkedIn, social media, or your portfolio.
